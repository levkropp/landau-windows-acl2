An oscillating reading combines a real signal, a scalar weight and a complex phase. Rational pairs let us compute an approximate phase exactly while proving that the approximation is close enough.

A pair can store complex coordinates

Represent x+iy by (x . y). Addition is coordinatewise; multiplication is (x,y)(u,v)=(xu−yv,xv+yu). A disk bound uses x²+y²≤r², not two separate coordinate bounds.

The pair shape also appears in intervals and cells. Its meaning depends on the construction: endpoints, complex coordinates and an address/weight pair are different mathematical objects.

Preserve length without a trigonometric oracle

R(t) = ((4−t²)/(4+t²), 4t/(4+t²))
Pₙ(t) = R(t/N)N.

The denominator is positive. The identity (4−t²)²+(4t)²=(4+t²)² proves that R(t) has length one. It approximates the desired angle; it is not generally exp(it).

For positive natural N and |t|≤N, the real bridge proves distance at most 2t²/N from Pₙ(t) to exp(it). At t=1,N=2, the exact point is (161/289,240/289). Unit length and a correct angle are separate properties.

Multiply three approximations

At log address u, the signal is Gz(exp(u)) and the phase is exp(−iHu). The log-signal constructor handles the non-rational exponential input by a nearby rational point and a proved transport allowance.

Let M cap the computed signal magnitude and K cap both kernel weights. Changing one factor at a time gives the sample-distance allowance

K δsignal + M δweight + MK δphase.

Choose coarse caps first, then tolerances. One component tolerance is ε/[3(1+factor)]; the signal tolerance is also capped at one. Since the scalar approximations are lower endpoints, their errors use full interval widths.

Budget the actual finite sum

A cell is an address paired with a nonnegative weight. The sampler multiplies signal, weight and phase; the finite-sum recursion adds those computed points. Assigning each of N weighted samples error ε/(N+1) gives a total evaluation allowance below ε.

The cell-list predicate does not prove mesh coverage. Real interpretation, mesh error and an omitted infinite tail still need their own arguments.
