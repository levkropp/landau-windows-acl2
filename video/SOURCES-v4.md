# Draft 04 — the value of extracting one essential step

The first six teaching chapters retain draft 03's narration and scene
methods. The transition beginning around 4:29 is revised to restate the RH
context, identify the Landau bridge, and explain the benefit of reproving
that inference with fewer foundational assumptions. The focus passage and
ending are also revised.

The mathematical sources and animation provenance are unchanged; see
[draft 03's source notes](SOURCES-v3.md). Two distinctions are preserved:

- Standard set-theoretic models of Lean's universes use suitable inaccessible
  cardinals. The narration does not identify Lean with ZFC or claim that
  every component mathematically requires those assumptions.
- A PA-strength Landau reduction is the project's **goal**, not an already
  completed result. The wider question of other reducible components, or a
  complete RH proof in PA, is discussed at the ending. This project does
  not settle those questions or establish a necessity result for the rest
  of the RH argument.
